## 1. 👴 Senior Citizen Flight Discounts & Airport Accommodations (Age 65+)

Senior traveler programs provide discounted commercial tariffs and critical airport escorts for passengers aged 65 and older. While digital checkout carts frequently exclude senior pricing buckets, telephone booking specialists can apply internal senior rate codes across domestic and international routes.

### Special Service Requests (SSRs) for Older Adults:
* **Curb-to-Gate Wheelchair Escort (WCHR / WCHS / WCHC):** Dedicated airport escorts assisting seniors through security checkpoints, terminal shuttles, and jet bridges at high-traffic hubs like Atlanta (ATL), Chicago (ORD), Denver (DEN), Dallas/Fort Worth (DFW), and New York (JFK).
* **Priority Pre-Boarding:** Early boarding opportunities to comfortably stow carry-on luggage and consult cabin attendants.
* **Forward Cabin Aisle Seating:** Optimal seat assignments near lavatories with level, unobstructed aisle access.
* **Flexible Change Privileges:** Compassionate penalty adjustments when travel dates shift due to medical considerations.

To compare carrier eligibility rules and verify discounted routes, consult the comprehensive [Senior Citizen Flight Discounts Guide](https://www.aironprice.com/senior-citizen-flight-discounts).

## 3. ⚖️ US DOT 2026 Flight Cancellation & Automatic Cash Refund Rules

Under United States Department of Transportation (DOT) federal regulations enacted in 2026, passengers are legally entitled to **full, prompt cash refunds** when an airline cancels a flight or makes significant schedule alterations, regardless of ticket class (including Basic Economy).

### Key Federal Consumer Rights:
1. **Significant Delay Thresholds:** A delay of 3+ hours for domestic itineraries or 6+ hours for international flights constitutes a significant delay eligible for cash refunds.
2. **Mandatory Cash / Original Form of Payment:** Airlines cannot force passengers into expiring travel vouchers or flight credits if the passenger declines alternative rebooking.
3. **Refund Processing Timeline:** Refunds must be processed within **7 business days** for credit card purchases and **20 business days** for other payment methods.
4. **Ancillary Service Refunds:** Wi-Fi, checked baggage, or seat selection fees for unrendered services must be reimbursed automatically.

To evaluate full refund eligibility or dispute rejected refund requests, refer to the [Flight Cancellation & Full Cash Refund Policy](https://www.aironprice.com/flight-cancellation-refund-policy).

## 4. ⏰ Missed Flight & Airport "Flat Tire" Standby Rules

Arriving late at the airport due to traffic, transit breakdowns, or unexpected delays does not necessarily void your ticket value. Most major commercial airlines maintain an informal or contractual **"Flat Tire Rule"**.

### Standard Same-Day Standby Directives:
* **The 2-Hour Window:** Arriving at the airline ticket counter within 2 hours after your scheduled departure typically permits rebooking on the next available standby flight without losing the ticket value.
* **Standby Priority:** Standby status is prioritized based on fare class and check-in timestamp.
* **Preserving Return Legs:** Calling an agent immediately upon missing a departure prevents automatic system cancellation of return connecting flights.

Review the full operational instructions in the [Missed Flight & Same-Day Standby Policy Guide](https://www.aironprice.com/missed-flight-same-day-standby-policy).

## 5. ✏️ Passenger Name Correction & Typo Rectification Guidelines

Transportation Security Administration (TSA) Secure Flight mandates that the passenger name on an airline ticket must match the traveler's government-issued photo ID (Real ID / Passport) exactly.

### Name Correction Protocols:
* **Minor Typos (Up to 3 Letters):** Simple spelling errors can generally be corrected without ticket reissuance fees when escalated via authorized phone desks.
* **Legal Name Adjustments:** Maiden-to-married name updates require documentation (marriage certificate / court decree).
* **Strict Non-Transferability:** Commercial flight tickets cannot be transferred to an entirely different individual.

To rectify airline boarding passes and prevent security gate denial, follow the official [Airline Ticket Name Correction Guide](https://www.aironprice.com/airline-ticket-name-correction-policy).
